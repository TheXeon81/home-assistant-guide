## 🇬🇧 English

**Home Assistant in the car with Android Auto: buttons, alerts when leaving and arriving**

Android Auto shows no dashboards: scripts in Favourites, car_ui notifications with two buttons, a 1 km zone for arrival and automations gated by the Android Auto sensor, over Tailscale.
